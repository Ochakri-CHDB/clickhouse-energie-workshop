# Measured results · ClickHouse Energy Workshop

Measured on October 14, 2026, full run from scratch (`./run_all.sh`, 3 min 36 s in total) on the test service:
**ClickHouse Cloud 26.6, AWS eu-central-1, 3 replicas of 64 GB (16 vCPU each)**, the configuration
recommended to participants. Durations read from `system.query_log` (server time). The data is simulated
and deterministic: volumes, rows read and results are identical for every participant.
The same numbers appear in the comments (`Measured`) under each statement of the SQL files.

> Orders of magnitude to remember for the slides
> - **1.5 billion** curve points generated and written in **67 s** (8 batches of 8.1 to 8.5 s, ~22 M points/s), **1.41 bytes per point**
> - One meter's curve over a month: **16,384 rows read out of 1.5 billion**, 5 to 55 ms
> - **3 replicas** on the same scan of 1.5 billion rows: **6.4 s → 3.0 s** (×2.1), without changing the SQL
> - One projection: the national peak goes from **1.7 s to 32 ms** (×54), **67,000 times fewer rows read**
> - Raw JSON compressed **×15**, typed curves **×18 to ×24**
> - A dashboard tile on gold: **2 to 30 ms**
